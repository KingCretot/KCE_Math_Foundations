Tap a card to flip it and learn the word. Tap the 🔊 to hear it read aloud.

Pattern
A sequence of numbers or shapes that follows a predictable rule.
Rule
The instruction that tells you how to get from one term to the next, like "add 3."
Term
One number or shape in a pattern. In 5, 8, 11 — each number is a term.
Growing Pattern
A pattern where the terms keep getting bigger, following the rule.
