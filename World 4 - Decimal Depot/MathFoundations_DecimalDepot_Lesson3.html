Nearest Whole

Nearest Tenth
Rounding a decimal so only one digit remains after the decimal point.
Round Up
If the next digit is 5 or more, round the digit before it up by one.
Round Down
If the next digit is 4 or less, keep the digit before it the same.

1️⃣ Rounding to the Nearest Whole Number

🧠 Watch Me Think

To round a decimal to the nearest whole number, look at the digit right after the decimal point (the tenths digit). If it's 5 or more, round UP. If it's 4 or less, round DOWN. For 3.7: the tenths digit is 7 (5 or more), so round UP to 4.

🖼️ Picture It

3.7  →  tenths digit 7 (≥5)  →  rounds up to 4

2️⃣ The Halfway Point Rule

🧠 Watch Me Think

What happens exactly at 5? The rule says round UP! For 6.5: the tenths digit is exactly 5, so we round UP to 7. This rule keeps rounding consistent every time.

🖼️ Picture It

6.5  →  tenths digit exactly 5  →  rounds up to 7

3️⃣ Rounding to the Nearest Tenth

🧠 Watch Me Think

You can also round to the nearest tenth — just look one place further, at the hundredths digit. For 2.65: the hundredths digit is 5 (5 or more), so round the tenths digit UP. 2.65 rounds to 2.7.

🖼️ Picture It

2.65 → hundredths digit 5 → rounds up to 2.7

🤝 Let's Try Together

For 4.82: hundredths digit is 2 (4 or less), so round DOWN. 4.82 rounds to 4.8.
