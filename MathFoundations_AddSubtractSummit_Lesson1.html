1️⃣ Lining Up the Columns

🧠 Watch Me Think

Before adding, we always line up the numbers so ones are under ones, and tens are under tens. This keeps everything organized so we add the right digits together.

🖼️ Picture It

  2 3
+ 1 4
——

Ones under ones (3, 4). Tens under tens (2, 1).

🤝 Let's Try Together

Line up 45 + 32. The 5 and 2 go in the ones column. The 4 and 3 go in the tens column.

2️⃣ Add Ones First, Then Tens

🧠 Watch Me Think

Add the ones column first: 3 + 4 = 7. Then add the tens column: 2 + 1 = 3. Put them together: 37. That's the sum of 23 + 14!

🖼️ Picture It

  2 3
+ 1 4
——
  3 7

As long as the ones digits add up to 9 or less, we don't need any special tricks yet — just add each column straight down. 34 + 44: ones 4+4=8, tens 3+4=7. Sum: 78.
