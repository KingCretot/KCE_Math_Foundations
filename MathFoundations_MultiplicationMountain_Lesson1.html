Factor
A number you multiply. In 6 × 7 = 42, the factors are 6 and 7.
Product
The answer to a multiplication problem. In 6 × 7 = 42, the product is 42.
Area Model
A rectangle split into smaller boxes to make multiplying easier — like tiling a floor in sections.
Partial Product
One small answer inside the area model. Add all the partial products to get the total.
