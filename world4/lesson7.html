1️⃣ Spotting Sharing Clue Words

🧠 Watch Me Think

Words like 'share equally' and 'each' signal division! 'Share 24 marbles equally among 4 friends. How many does each friend get?' → 24 ÷ 4 = 6.

🖼️ Picture It

"share equally" · "each" → DIVIDE

🤝 Let's Try Together

'Split 30 cookies equally among 5 kids.' Share equally means divide: 30 ÷ 5 = 6 cookies each.

2️⃣ Spotting Grouping Clue Words

🧠 Watch Me Think

Phrases like 'how many groups' and 'per' also signal division — but this time you're finding the number of groups, not the size of each group. 'There are 36 apples, 6 per basket. How many baskets?' → 36 ÷ 6 = 6.

🖼️ Picture It

"per" · "how many groups" → DIVIDE

🤝 Let's Try Together

'40 chairs, 5 chairs per row. How many rows?' 40 ÷ 5 = 8 rows.

3️⃣ Mixed Division Word Problems

🧠 Watch Me Think

Always ask: am I finding the size of each group, or the number of groups? Either way, if a total is being split into equal parts, it's division. 'A baker has 45 muffins in 9 equal trays. How many muffins per tray?' → 45 ÷ 9 = 5.

🖼️ Picture It

Total split into equal parts? → DIVIDE

🤝 Let's Try Together

'56 seats, 7 rows. How many seats per row?' 56 ÷ 7 = 8 seats per row.
