🗺️ Mission Briefing — Everything You Learned

Before the final challenge, let's remember every skill on this mountain.

1️⃣ Area Model

Break big multiplication into easy chunks: 23 × 4 = (20 × 4) + (3 × 4).

2️⃣ Factors & Multiples

Factors divide evenly into a number. Multiples come from skip counting.

3️⃣ Times as Many

A phrase like "4 times as many" always means multiply.

4️⃣ Splitting & Remainders

Division splits into equal groups. Leftovers become the remainder.

5️⃣ Order of Operations

Parentheses first, then multiply/divide, then add/subtract.

6️⃣ Patterns

Every pattern follows a rule, like "add 3" or "multiply by 2".

Quotient
