1️⃣ Equivalent Fractions

Fractions that look different but equal the same amount, like 1/2 and 2/4.

2️⃣ Comparing Fractions

Same denominator? Compare tops. Same top? Fewer pieces wins. Different? Find a common denominator.

Same denominator fractions combine by adding or subtracting just the numerators.

4️⃣ Mixed Numbers

A whole number and a fraction together, like 2 1/4.

Multiply straight across: numerators together, denominators together.

6️⃣ Decimals

Tenths and hundredths are just fractions written a different way.

1️⃣ Equivalent Fractions + Simplifying

🧠 Watch Me Think

8/12 and 2/3 are the SAME amount — they are equivalent. To go from 8/12 to 2/3, divide top and bottom by 4. That is simplifying.

🖼️ Picture It

= 2/3
