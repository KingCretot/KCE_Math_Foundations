1️⃣ What Is a Turn-Around Fact?

🧠 Watch Me Think

Multiplication has a cool rule: you can flip the order of the factors and get the SAME product! 3 × 4 = 12, and 4 × 3 = 12 too. These are called turn-around facts.

🖼️ Picture It

3 × 4 = 4 × 3 = 12

🤝 Let's Try Together

What's the turn-around fact for 6 × 2? It's 2 × 6 — and both equal 12.

2️⃣ Using Turn-Arounds to Pick the Easier Fact

🧠 Watch Me Think

Sometimes one order is easier to calculate than the other! 9 × 2 might feel harder than 2 × 9 — but with the doubling trick, 2 × 9 is easy: double 9 = 18. Same answer either way, so pick whichever is easier for YOU.

🖼️ Picture It

9 × 2 = 2 × 9 = 18
(double 9 is easier than counting nine 2s!)

🤝 Let's Try Together

For 10 × 6, it might be easier to think of it as 6 × 10 — add a zero to 6 to get 60.

Turn-around facts cut the number of facts you need to memorize almost in half! If you know 6 × 8 = 48, you automatically know 8 × 6 = 48 too.
